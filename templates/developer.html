Hi, I'm Mohak.

I'm a Computer Science student interested in machine learning, data science, and building things that seem like good ideas at 2 AM.

Apart from programming, I've always been fascinated by outer space, astronomy, and cosmology. There's something about the sheer scale of the universe, distant galaxies, black holes, and the possibility of worlds beyond our own that I find incredibly interesting.

I enjoy experimenting with technology, learning how things work, and turning random ideas into actual projects.

Exoplanet Hunter is where two of my biggest interests came together: machine learning and astronomy. What started as a project using NASA's Kepler dataset eventually turned into an entire website dedicated to exploring distant worlds.

I'm still learning, still experimenting, and probably already thinking about what to build next.
